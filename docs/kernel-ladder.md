# Kernel ladder

Chamfera's B-rep kernel is written from scratch. Each rung is finished, with tests against analytic values, before the next starts. Rules: `skills/chamfera-kernel/SKILL.md`.

| Rung | What | Done when | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| K0 | Geometry basics: points, vectors, frames, transforms, tolerances, robust orientation predicates | Unit tests incl. near-degenerate cases | Done: `Frame` (origin planes XY/XZ/YZ), `Transform` (rigid and affine), exact `orient2d`/`orient3d`/`incircle` after Shewchuk, in `crates/geom` |
| K1 | Curves: line, circle/arc, ellipse; evaluation, derivatives, closest point, curve-curve intersection in a plane | Analytic intersection tests | Done: line, segment, circle, arc, ellipse, elliptical arc in 3D (evaluation, derivatives, length, closest point, box, reverse); planar line/segment/circle/arc intersections with tangent and overlap cases, in `crates/kernel` |
| K2 | Topology: body / shell / face / loop / edge / vertex with half-edges; Euler operators; validity check | Euler-Poincare and orientation checks on hand-built cube, prism, cylinder | Done: half-edge `Body` in typed-id arenas with curves (K1) and planes by id; MVFS/KVFS, MEV/KEV, MEF/KEF, KEMR/MEKR, KFMRH/MFKRH; topology and geometry validity checks; cuboid, prism with holes (genus) and cylinder topology (lateral surface waits for K3); exact planar area and volume; validated serde, in `crates/kernel/src/topology` |
| K3 | Surfaces: plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus; point/normal evaluation, closest point, inside-face test | Analytic tests | Done: `Surface` with plane, cylinder, cone, sphere and torus on a `Frame` (point, partial derivatives, normal, projection to (u, v) with seams and singular points, closest point); faces mapped into (u, v) (loop images with seams and pole steps) for an inside-face test and an orientation check; `Body::validate` checks curved faces; exact area and volume of faces on planes and surfaces of revolution by Green's theorem in (u, v) (closed form for edges along or around the axis, adaptive Gauss-Legendre otherwise); `Body::cylinder_topology` is a valid solid, in `crates/kernel/src/surface.rs` and `crates/kernel/src/topology` |
| K4 | Sweeps: extrude and revolve a planar profile (lines + arcs, with holes) into a valid solid | Volume, area, centre of mass of box, plate-with-holes, cylinder, cone, torus match closed forms | Done: kernel `Profile` (lines, arcs, full circles; outer loop and holes; validated, orientation fixed automatically); `extrude` (either way, and symmetric), `extrude_tapered` (a draft angle: side faces become tilted planes and cones, from the constant-distance offset of the profile; sharp corners at arcs, and tapers that make an edge vanish or the profile overlap itself, are refused) and `revolve` (partial or full turn; profiles may touch the axis at vertices and along edges) to valid bodies with plane, cylinder, cone, sphere and torus faces and role names (`side 3`, `top`, `start cap`, ...); volume and area match closed forms and Pappus' theorem; centre of mass deferred to the oracle parts (M3), full revolves that would enclose a void refused, in `crates/kernel/src/profile.rs` and `crates/kernel/src/sweep` |
| K4+ | Mass properties and measuring (beside the ladder, on K3/K4/K6) | Centre of mass and inertia of box, cylinder, plate with a hole, sphere and torus match closed forms; distances and angles between boxes | Done: `Body::mass_properties` (volume, area, mass, centre of mass, inertia tensor about it, principal moments and axes by cyclic Jacobi) by the divergence theorem after Mirtich (1996), each face's flux taken by Green's theorem in its `(u, v)` domain with Gauss-Legendre quadrature on the exact surface, in `crates/kernel/src/mass.rs`; `closest_points` (vertex, edge, face, body, free point: exact for straight edges and flat faces, alternating closest points on curved ones), `angle_between` (straight edges, flat faces), edge length and radius, face radius, in `crates/kernel/src/distance.rs` |
| K4+ | Sheet metal (beside the ladder, on K4 and K7) | A base, flanges and their flat pattern match hand-derived volumes and lengths | Done: `Sheet` in `crates/kernel/src/sheet`: a base plate (extrude), flanges on straight edges between a sheet face and a side face one thickness wide (the bend and wall as one extruded profile of exact cylinders and planes, joined by K7; the sheet trimmed back by the setback `(R + T) tan(θ/2)` or `R tan(θ/2)` for the bend position; rectangular or obround reliefs where a flange stops short of its edge), and the flat pattern by unrolling each flat piece through `-θ` about its bend axis and along by the bend allowance `θ (R + K T)` (standard sheet metal practice), the pieces joined into one planar solid whose outline the engine exports as DXF R12 with the drawing crate's writer. Obround reliefs close to a bend need crossing cylinders (K10) and are refused there |
| K5 | Tessellation: faces to triangles within a chord tolerance; watertight meshes | Mesh volume converges to the exact volume; no cracks | Done for planar faces: edges discretised once (sagitta and angle bounds) and shared by both faces, faces triangulated in their surface's (u, v) domain by hole bridging and ear clipping with exact `orient2d`, then Delaunay flips; `tessellate` gives a closed `TriMesh` with a face per triangle and true edge polylines, within a triangle budget, in `crates/kernel/src/tessellate`. Curved faces done: cylinder, cone, sphere and torus domains in scaled kernel parameters, seams unwrapped by the period, poles and apexes walked as runs of copies of one mesh vertex (fans closed, no folds), refinement by side bisection and centroids (chord, normal turn along sides, quarter-turn span), then smoothing and flips judged in space; every sweep closed and consistently oriented, mesh volume within `A · tol` and area within `tol (2 ∫|H| dA + L)` of the exact values, converging linearly |
| K6 | Surface-surface intersection: plane/plane, plane/cylinder, plane/cone, plane/sphere, cylinder/cylinder (coaxial and orthogonal first) | Analytic intersection curves | Done: `intersect_surfaces` returns exact K1 curves (lines, rays from a cone's apex, circles, ellipses) in an explicit result (`Empty`, `Coincident`, `Curves`, `Tangent` with crossing/tangent contact per curve and isolated points, `Unsupported`): plane/plane; plane/cylinder (lines, tangent line, circle, ellipse); plane/sphere; plane/cone (circle, ellipse, apex, one or two generators); plane/torus perpendicular to or through the axis; parallel and coaxial cylinders; equal-radius cylinders with meeting axes (two ellipses); any two coaxial surfaces of revolution (all sphere pairs, sphere/cylinder, cylinder/cone, cone/cone, torus pairs) by meridians. Unsupported until K10 (documented in `UnsupportedIntersection`): parabolic and hyperbolic cone sections, other torus sections, skew or unequal non-parallel cylinders, other non-coaxial curved pairs (empty when a bounding sphere proves it). Surfaces of linear extrusion (K10, branch `kernel/spline-booleans`, `crates/kernel/src/ssi/extrusion.rs`): a plane along the extrusion cuts rulings at the roots of the curve against its trace line, a plane across it the curve at its height, an oblique plane an exact B-spline (the affine image of the curve's control points, The NURBS Book §4.2); a cylinder along the extrusion and an extrusion along the same direction meet it in rulings at the crossings of the plane curves; coincident when one curve lies on the other; a cone, a sphere or torus reaching it, or a cylinder or extrusion along another direction, is `Unsupported` (`Spline`). Curves against an extrusion are projected into its plane; B-splines against the analytic surfaces are roots of the signed distance. Both by convex-hull subdivision and Newton (Lane and Riesenfeld 1980; Patrikalakis and Maekawa §5.6-5.8), a leaf holding at most one root (small with disjoint sampled tangent cones, after Sederberg and Meyers 1988, or flat within the tolerance), touching points placed by bisection where the tangents align, at most 200 000 intervals and 96 halvings per call. One tolerance band per decision (`LINEAR_TOLERANCE`, `ANGULAR_TOLERANCE`), so tangent cases flip consistently at r ± tolerance. Also curve-surface intersection (`intersect_curve_surface`: lines, segments, circles, arcs, ellipses against every surface by substitution into the implicit equation, parameters, points and tangency) and `classify_point` (ray casting with the K3 inside-face test, re-cast along fixed fallback directions on degenerate rays), in `crates/kernel/src/ssi` |
| K7 | Booleans (union, subtract, intersect) on planar and analytic solids: face splitting, classification, sewing | Oracle parts; tangent, coincident and touching cases | Done: `union`, `subtract`, `intersect` (and `boolean` with a `BooleanOp`) in `crates/kernel/src/boolean`, by boundary evaluation (Requicha and Voelcker; Mantyla ch. 15): every face pair intersected with K6, the curves trimmed to both faces (K6 curve-surface against surfaces that meet each face exactly along its boundary edges, K3 inside-face test), coincident faces imprinting each other's edges, edges split at every new vertex in one shared registry, each face traced into pieces (outer loops and holes, in `(u, v)` on curved faces), pieces classified at an interior point (K6 `classify_point`, with the outward normal deciding on-boundary pieces), selected per operation and sewn: non-manifold contact edges paired by material wedge, coplanar neighbours and pieces of one curved face merged, collinear leftovers joined, touching solids given their own vertices, the result assembled from its face list (`Body::from_face_list`) and validated. Handles face-on-face contact (a boss on a plate unites into one shell with no internal face), cuts flush with a face, coaxial and equal coincident cylinders, through and blind holes, edge-on-face, edge-edge and corner touches, disjoint unions (one shell per lump), voids (a shell with inward faces inside a lump, now accepted by `validate`) and an explicit `KernelError::EmptyResult`. Result faces keep their operand face's name (persistent naming begins: `top` of the plate stays `top`), intersection edges are named `<face A> x <face B>`. Refused with `KernelError::Unsupported` (never a broken body): face pairs whose intersection K6 reports unsupported (e.g. crossing cylinders of unequal radius). Spline sides (surfaces of linear extrusion) take part (branch `kernel/spline-booleans`): planar B-spline edge carriers, crossings through the spline's extrusion (planar faces), its plane and the plane across a ruling (spline faces), pieces traced in `(u, v)` with `u` along the curve, exact planar inside tests on spline edges, oblique sections measured along their own parameter, faces tangent along a ruling ordered by a chord into each surface; tested against closed forms (half an ellipse plate 250π, a centred hole 460π, crossed ellipses 4ab·atan(b/a)·h, the arch halved, an oblique cut by Cavalieri, a skimming plane's sliver, touching along a ruling) and by the volume identities on 24 random spline, box and cylinder pairs (0 refusals in a 1400-pair sweep, `CHAMFERA_SPLINE_PAIRS=700`). A randomized test checks V(A ∪ B) + V(A ∩ B) = V(A) + V(B) and V(A − B) = V(A) − V(A ∩ B) to 1e-9: 0 refusals over 900 box operations (grid and continuous) and 600 with vertical cylinders; with cylinders along any axis, 30 of 300 refused (crossing cylinders), all cleanly |
| K8 | Persistent naming of every created face, edge and vertex | Names survive a parameter change in the oracle parts | Done for splits and renames (branch `kernel/k8-naming`): history names `feature:role` as before (generative naming after Capoyleas, Chen and Hoffmann 1996; Kripac 1997), resolved by one function, `chamfera_doc::naming::resolve`, that every consumer uses (sketches on faces, extrude to a face, holes, fillets and chamfers, shells, face edits, construction geometry, mirrors on faces, joints, projections, sheet flanges, CAM, measuring, section planes, picking). Pieces of a split face or edge get unique names `<name>#k`, ordered by their anchor point (x, then y, then z on a 1e-6 mm grid). A reference keeps a signature (format 27, `Document::references`: shape, normal or axis, a point, neighbouring face names). A whole name means every piece to a consumer that takes many (fillet, shell, face edit, drill) and to one that needs one surface when the pieces share it (sketch plane, hole plane, extrude to face); otherwise the piece containing the point wins (or the clearly nearest, at most half the next distance), then the `#k` number; else a clear error: `the face "2:top" split into 2 pieces; pick one: "2:top#1", "2:top#2"`. A name that is gone is found again by its signature (same shape and direction, through the point, sharing a neighbour), after the geometric remedies Marcheix and Pierra (2002) survey. Pattern copies now name their edges and vertices per copy (`6:3 top 0`), so a fillet on one copy no longer rounds every copy. Tests: a slot moved across a sketch's face, a fillet on a whole and on one piece of an edge split by a moved slot, holes on a plate cut in two that grows, a shell's open face through a moved notch and a thicker plate, fillets on pattern copies as the count changes, example designs at their hand-derived volumes, hostile signatures and references. Limits: piece numbers without a signature follow geometry order, so a new piece before them renumbers them; signatures record a point once and do not track large moves of the whole face (the nearest-piece rule then decides or reports); joints and CAM operations keep no signatures (they resolve by name and piece number); a circular pattern copy keeps its index when the count changes, not its angle |
| K9 | STEP AP214/AP242 export of B-rep (then import) | Round-trip of oracle parts | Export done for planar bodies: AP214 (`AUTOMOTIVE_DESIGN`) Part 21 with product structure, mm/radian units, `ADVANCED_BREP_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` of `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`s (`PLANE`; `LINE`, `CIRCLE`, `ELLIPSE` edges), shortest round-trip reals, escaped strings; a minimal Part 21 reader checks our output (counts, references, closed-shell edge use, bit-exact vertices), in `crates/io/src/step`. Curved surfaces done: `CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE`, `CONICAL_SURFACE` (positive semi-angle, the frame turned over for cones narrowing toward +z), `SPHERICAL_SURFACE`, `TOROIDAL_SURFACE` and `DEGENERATE_TOROIDAL_SURFACE` for spindle and lemon tori, with `same_sense` set against Part 42's `σ_u × σ_v` (flipped on the lemon part); every K4 solid exports and the reader checks each face's surface and placement. Import done (`step::import`, `body.import_step`): Part 21 edition 2 and 3, complex instances, mm/m/inch and degree units; `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` of `ADVANCED_FACE`/`FACE_SURFACE` on the five analytic surfaces with `LINE`, `CIRCLE`, `ELLIPSE` edges, built by `Body::from_boundary` and validated; every K4 solid round-trips with the same topology and measures to 1e-9. Refused per solid for now: B-splines (K10), voids, faceted B-reps; assembly placements are not applied |
| K10 | NURBS curves and surfaces (The NURBS Book), and their intersections | Analytic and convergence tests | Curves done (branch `kernel/k10-splines`): rational `BSplineCurve2`/`BSplineCurve3` in `crates/geom/src/nurbs` (Piegl and Tiller): evaluation and derivatives (A2.1-A2.3, A3.2, A4.1-A4.2), knot insertion (A5.1), splitting and segments, the control-point box (convex hull), closest point by Newton (§6.1) with a box-pruned subdivision fallback, arc length and Green area moments by adaptive Gauss-Legendre per knot span, global interpolation with chord-length parameters, averaged knots and optional end tangents (A9.1, §9.2.2), closed fit and control splines; hostile input refused (degree 1..=7, knot vector rules, weights in [1e-6, 1e6], at most 1024 control and 256 fit points). Tested exactly: the rational quadratic (1,0), (1,1), (0,1), weights 1, √2/2, 1 is a quarter circle to 1e-12 with length π/2; degree 1 is its polyline; insertion and splitting leave the curve unchanged; interpolation hits its fit points. Kernel: `Curve::BSpline` edges, `ProfileEdge::Spline` (area by quadrature, π for the rational circle; spline self-crossing and crossing checks on fine chords), and `Surface::Extrusion` (surface of linear extrusion, `crates/kernel/src/extrusion.rs`) for the side faces of `extrude`: validation, (u, v) images, tessellation domain, exact-to-quadrature area and volume by Green's theorem (extruded volume = profile area × height, tested on the arch, the rational cylinder and a plate with a spline hole), rigid moves, serde, STEP export as `B_SPLINE_CURVE_WITH_KNOTS` (rational as the complex instance) and `SURFACE_OF_LINEAR_EXTRUSION`. Refused with `KernelError::Unsupported`: fillets and chamfers on bodies with spline faces, revolving splines, extruding them off the sketch normal, spline sides against cones, spheres, tori and non-parallel cylinders or extrusions; STEP import of B-splines. Booleans on spline sides done (see K6 and K7). Pending: NURBS surfaces, marching for general spline intersections |
| K11 | Edge blends: constant-radius fillet and chamfer between planar and analytic faces, then rolling-ball blends in general | Fillet volumes against closed forms | First part done: `fillet` (constant radius) and `chamfer` (equal distance) in `crates/kernel/src/blend`. Straight edges between two planar faces, convex (removes material) or concave (adds it), singly, in chains and at three-edge corners: exact `Cylinder` blends with circle or ellipse ends on end faces, chamfer bevel planes; two blends at a corner meet along a bisector-plane ellipse (fillet) or segment (chamfer); three meet in an exact `Sphere` octant patch or a triangular corner face. Circular rims between a plane and a perpendicular cylinder (hole or boss): exact `Torus` fillet, `Cone` chamfer, with a meridian seam. Neighbouring faces keep their names (`top` stays `top`); blend faces are `fillet of <edge>` / `chamfer of <edge>`, corners `fillet corner at <vertex>`. Refused cleanly: hostile sizes, a blend consuming a face or edge or crossing a face, edges on other surfaces, vertices with more than three edges or curved neighbours, mixed convex/concave corners, unequal setbacks at a two-blend corner. Tested against closed forms (box edge, rounded box by Steiner's formula, mitred top chain, sphere-octant corner, oblique ends by Cavalieri, L-bracket concave edge, hole and boss rims by Pappus). Pending: variable radius, blends on curved faces in general, setback vertex blends, overlap with distant faces |
| K12 | Shell, draft, sweep along a path, loft | Oracle parts | Shell (first part) done: `shell(body, open_faces, thickness)` in `crates/kernel/src/shell`, after Forsyth ("Shelling and offsetting bodies", 1995) and Stroud (ch. on offsets): an offset body with the solid's topology (planes moved along their normal, cylinders change radius by `t`, cones keep axis and half-angle with radius changed by `t / cos α`; open faces stay put; each vertex moved by Gauss-Newton onto the offset surfaces of its faces, least-norm where seams or tangent faces fix fewer directions; edges rebuilt as segments or coaxial circles and checked against both offset surfaces), then subtracted by K7 (an open face is shared with the same normal, so it drops out; no open face leaves a void). A negative thickness shells outward (offset minus solid). The solid's faces keep their names; walls are `shell of <face>`. Refused: zero or hostile thickness, every face open, several lumps or voids (`InvalidInput`/`Unsupported`); spheres, tori, elliptical edges, vertices on an axis, cones past their apex (`Unsupported`); a thickness that makes an offset face or edge vanish or turn over, or walls cross (the shell's volume must equal solid minus offset) (`InvalidInput`). Tested against closed forms: tray and closed hollow box, cup from a cylinder, plate with a hole, cone frustum along its slant, block with filleted vertical edges (rounded-rectangle areas), outward shell. Pending: spheres and tori, draft, sweep along a path, loft. Sweep along a path done: `sweep_path` in `crates/kernel/src/sweep_path`: a profile (with holes) along a tangent-continuous chain of lines and arcs, kept perpendicular by a rotation-minimising frame (exact for lines and arcs), each piece an exact extrude or revolve (planes, cylinders, cones, spheres, tori; a circle along an arc is a torus piece) glued cap to cap without surface intersection; refused: corners, profiles reaching an arc's axis, closed or branching paths, and (conservatively, by a 2ρ tube bound) anything that might cross itself; tested by Pappus (quarter bend, L path, pipe). Loft done for exact cases: `loft` in `crates/kernel/src/loft` joins 2 or more hole-free profiles on parallel planes with equal edge counts by ruled faces: parallel lines give planar trapezoids, coaxial arcs over the same angles give cones or cylinders; tested against frustum and cone-frustum volumes and Simpson for a slot. Refused with `Unsupported`: skew lines (hyperbolic paraboloid, no surface kind yet), oblique cones, line against arc, holes; general lofts wait for K10. Direct face edits done: `offset_faces`, `delete_faces`, `move_faces` in `crates/kernel/src/face_edit` (Stroud, local operations; Forsyth 1995): the body copied into a draft of faces and loops, surfaces changed (planes moved or turned, cylinders, spheres and tori change radius, cones by `d / cos α`) or the topology changed (a fillet or chamfer face collapsed back to the edge between its neighbours by merging its rails and collapsing its ends, the inverse of the blend's expansion; a hole, pocket or boss removed by dropping the inner loops it leaves), then every changed vertex solved by Gauss-Newton onto its faces' implicit surfaces and every changed edge given the exact segment, coaxial circle or K6 line, circle or ellipse of its two faces; validated, and refused when `V(new) ≠ V(old) + V(new − old) − V(old − new)` (faces crossing). Refused: faces vanishing, turning over or crossing, faces moved off tangent neighbours, other curves (K10), other deletions. Tested: box top ±, hole wall radius, filleted-block corner, hole rim chamfer and fillet, blind hole fill, tilted top. |
| K12s | Surface bodies: open shells, swept surfaces, patch, stitch, trim (beside the ladder, on K2, K4, K7) | Surface extrude of 50 x 30 by 10 has area 1600 and 8 rim edges; a revolved line is 2πrh; patching both ends and stitching gives 15000; a plane through the middle halves a cylinder surface | Done (branch `surface/basics`): openings of open shells are boundary faces (flagged, no surface) so every edge stays two-sided (boundary half-edges as in Botsch et al., *Polygon Mesh Processing*, §2.5); `Body::faces`/`summary` hide them, `boundary_edges`, `is_open`, `validate_as(Validation::Surface)`, `volume` and mass refused; `Body::from_face_list_open` chains missing uses into opening loops; `without_faces`; `surface_extrude`/`surface_revolve`/`surface_sweep` (solid sweeps of the profile, or of an open chain closed by a chord or two lines, minus caps and closing faces), `patch_profile`, `patch_edges`, `stitch` (vertex and rim-edge merging, whole-body reversal by 2-colouring), `trim_by_plane`/`trim_by_solid` (the K7 split, classify and an open sew), in `crates/kernel/src/surfacing` |
