# Kernel ladder

Chamfera's B-rep kernel is written from scratch. Each rung is finished, with tests against analytic values, before the next starts. Rules: `skills/chamfera-kernel/SKILL.md`.

| Rung | What | Done when | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| K0 | Geometry basics: points, vectors, frames, transforms, tolerances, robust orientation predicates | Unit tests incl. near-degenerate cases | Done: `Frame` (origin planes XY/XZ/YZ), `Transform` (rigid and affine), exact `orient2d`/`orient3d`/`incircle` after Shewchuk, in `crates/geom` |
| K1 | Curves: line, circle/arc, ellipse; evaluation, derivatives, closest point, curve-curve intersection in a plane | Analytic intersection tests | Done: line, segment, circle, arc, ellipse, elliptical arc in 3D (evaluation, derivatives, length, closest point, box, reverse); planar line/segment/circle/arc intersections with tangent and overlap cases, in `crates/kernel` |
| K2 | Topology: body / shell / face / loop / edge / vertex with half-edges; Euler operators; validity check | Euler-Poincare and orientation checks on hand-built cube, prism, cylinder | Done: half-edge `Body` in typed-id arenas with curves (K1) and planes by id; MVFS/KVFS, MEV/KEV, MEF/KEF, KEMR/MEKR, KFMRH/MFKRH; topology and geometry validity checks; cuboid, prism with holes (genus) and cylinder topology (lateral surface waits for K3); exact planar area and volume; validated serde, in `crates/kernel/src/topology` |
| K3 | Surfaces: plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus; point/normal evaluation, closest point, inside-face test | Analytic tests | Done: `Surface` with plane, cylinder, cone, sphere and torus on a `Frame` (point, partial derivatives, normal, projection to (u, v) with seams and singular points, closest point); faces mapped into (u, v) (loop images with seams and pole steps) for an inside-face test and an orientation check; `Body::validate` checks curved faces; exact area and volume of faces on planes and surfaces of revolution by Green's theorem in (u, v) (closed form for edges along or around the axis, adaptive Gauss-Legendre otherwise); `Body::cylinder_topology` is a valid solid, in `crates/kernel/src/surface.rs` and `crates/kernel/src/topology` |
| K4 | Sweeps: extrude and revolve a planar profile (lines + arcs, with holes) into a valid solid | Volume, area, centre of mass of box, plate-with-holes, cylinder, cone, torus match closed forms | Done: kernel `Profile` (lines, arcs, full circles; outer loop and holes; validated, orientation fixed automatically); `extrude` (either way, and symmetric) and `revolve` (partial or full turn; profiles may touch the axis at vertices and along edges) to valid bodies with plane, cylinder, cone, sphere and torus faces and role names (`side 3`, `top`, `start cap`, ...); volume and area match closed forms and Pappus' theorem; centre of mass deferred to the oracle parts (M3), full revolves that would enclose a void refused, in `crates/kernel/src/profile.rs` and `crates/kernel/src/sweep` |
| K5 | Tessellation: faces to triangles within a chord tolerance; watertight meshes | Mesh volume converges to the exact volume; no cracks | Done for planar faces: edges discretised once (sagitta and angle bounds) and shared by both faces, faces triangulated in their surface's (u, v) domain by hole bridging and ear clipping with exact `orient2d`, then Delaunay flips; `tessellate` gives a closed `TriMesh` with a face per triangle and true edge polylines, within a triangle budget, in `crates/kernel/src/tessellate`. Curved faces wait for K3: the (u, v) path, seam unwrapping and interior refinement are in place, each surface plugs in a `SurfaceDomain` (see the module documentation) |
| K6 | Surface-surface intersection: plane/plane, plane/cylinder, plane/cone, plane/sphere, cylinder/cylinder (coaxial and orthogonal first) | Analytic intersection curves | Done: `intersect_surfaces` returns exact K1 curves (lines, rays from a cone's apex, circles, ellipses) in an explicit result (`Empty`, `Coincident`, `Curves`, `Tangent` with crossing/tangent contact per curve and isolated points, `Unsupported`): plane/plane; plane/cylinder (lines, tangent line, circle, ellipse); plane/sphere; plane/cone (circle, ellipse, apex, one or two generators); plane/torus perpendicular to or through the axis; parallel and coaxial cylinders; equal-radius cylinders with meeting axes (two ellipses); any two coaxial surfaces of revolution (all sphere pairs, sphere/cylinder, cylinder/cone, cone/cone, torus pairs) by meridians. Unsupported until K10 (documented in `UnsupportedIntersection`): parabolic and hyperbolic cone sections, other torus sections, skew or unequal non-parallel cylinders, other non-coaxial curved pairs (empty when a bounding sphere proves it). One tolerance band per decision (`LINEAR_TOLERANCE`, `ANGULAR_TOLERANCE`), so tangent cases flip consistently at r ± tolerance. Also curve-surface intersection (`intersect_curve_surface`: lines, segments, circles, arcs, ellipses against every surface by substitution into the implicit equation, parameters, points and tangency) and `classify_point` (ray casting with the K3 inside-face test, re-cast along fixed fallback directions on degenerate rays), in `crates/kernel/src/ssi` |
| K7 | Booleans (union, subtract, intersect) on planar and analytic solids: face splitting, classification, sewing | Oracle parts; tangent, coincident and touching cases | Done: `union`, `subtract`, `intersect` (and `boolean` with a `BooleanOp`) in `crates/kernel/src/boolean`, by boundary evaluation (Requicha and Voelcker; Mantyla ch. 15): every face pair intersected with K6, the curves trimmed to both faces (K6 curve-surface against surfaces that meet each face exactly along its boundary edges, K3 inside-face test), coincident faces imprinting each other's edges, edges split at every new vertex in one shared registry, each face traced into pieces (outer loops and holes, in `(u, v)` on curved faces), pieces classified at an interior point (K6 `classify_point`, with the outward normal deciding on-boundary pieces), selected per operation and sewn: non-manifold contact edges paired by material wedge, coplanar neighbours and pieces of one curved face merged, collinear leftovers joined, touching solids given their own vertices, the result assembled from its face list (`Body::from_face_list`) and validated. Handles face-on-face contact (a boss on a plate unites into one shell with no internal face), cuts flush with a face, coaxial and equal coincident cylinders, through and blind holes, edge-on-face, edge-edge and corner touches, disjoint unions (one shell per lump), voids (a shell with inward faces inside a lump, now accepted by `validate`) and an explicit `KernelError::EmptyResult`. Result faces keep their operand face's name (persistent naming begins: `top` of the plate stays `top`), intersection edges are named `<face A> x <face B>`. Refused with `KernelError::Unsupported` (never a broken body): face pairs whose intersection K6 reports unsupported (e.g. crossing cylinders of unequal radius). A randomized test checks V(A ∪ B) + V(A ∩ B) = V(A) + V(B) and V(A − B) = V(A) − V(A ∩ B) to 1e-9: 0 refusals over 900 box operations (grid and continuous) and 600 with vertical cylinders; with cylinders along any axis, 30 of 300 refused (crossing cylinders), all cleanly |
| K8 | Persistent naming of every created face, edge and vertex | Names survive a parameter change in the oracle parts | |
| K9 | STEP AP214/AP242 export of B-rep (then import) | Round-trip of oracle parts | Export done for planar bodies: AP214 (`AUTOMOTIVE_DESIGN`) Part 21 with product structure, mm/radian units, `ADVANCED_BREP_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION` of `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP`s (`PLANE`; `LINE`, `CIRCLE`, `ELLIPSE` edges), shortest round-trip reals, escaped strings; a minimal Part 21 reader checks our output (counts, references, closed-shell edge use, bit-exact vertices), in `crates/io/src/step`. K3 surfaces wait in `write::surface`. Import pending |

| K11 | Edge blends: constant-radius fillet and chamfer between planar and analytic faces, then rolling-ball blends in general | Fillet volumes against closed forms | |
| K12 | Shell, draft, sweep along a path, loft | Oracle parts | |
